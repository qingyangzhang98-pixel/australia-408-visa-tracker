## Primary financial evidence

**CSC Award / Funding Letter**

The CSC documents should ideally show:

- Applicant name
- Funding period
- Scholarship / stipend
- Duration corresponding to the research stay

## Supplementary evidence

Recommended:

- [ ] Recent personal bank statements

Suggested approach:

- Use a normal personal account
- Provide recent transaction history
- Use the bank statement only as **supplementary evidence**
- Do not make unnecessary last-minute large deposits purely for the visa application

Overall financial logic:

> **CSC is the primary source of funding. Personal savings provide supplementary financial capacity.**

# ✉️ Cover Letter — Core Logic

Suggested structure:

### 1. Applicant background
Current PhD candidate and academic background.

### 2. Purpose of visit
Temporary research activities at Monash University.

### 3. Monash arrangement
Host department, Supervisor and research arrangement.

### 4. Research relationship
Explain how the Monash research directly supports the current PhD project.

### 5. Why 24 months
Explain why the research activities require the proposed duration.

### 6. Funding
Explain CSC sponsorship and supplementary personal funds.

### 7. Temporary nature of stay
Explain the plan to continue / complete the PhD and academic work after the research period.

Target length:

**1–2 pages**
